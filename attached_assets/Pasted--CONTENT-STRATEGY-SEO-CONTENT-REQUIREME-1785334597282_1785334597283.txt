==========================================================
CONTENT STRATEGY & SEO CONTENT REQUIREMENTS (MANDATORY)
==========================================================

Content is one of the highest priorities for this project. Every page must be written with a human-first, SEO-first, and AI-first approach to maximize organic rankings, AI search visibility, and conversions.

### Content Requirements

- Every important page must contain **at least 2,000 words** of unique, high-quality, well-researched, and engaging content (or more where appropriate based on search intent).
- Content must be 100% original, plagiarism-free, and written specifically for this website.
- Do not use duplicate or thin content.
- Do not use keyword stuffing or AI-generated filler text.
- Every page should demonstrate Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (EEAT).

### Existing Content Migration

- Analyze the existing website thoroughly.
- Migrate and improve all existing content.
- Rewrite and optimize content where necessary while preserving the original meaning and SEO value.
- Ensure no existing valuable content is lost during the migration.

### Existing Blogs

- Migrate **all existing blog posts** from the current website.
- Preserve existing blog URLs wherever possible.
- Improve and update blog content where necessary.
- Maintain blog categories and tags.
- Preserve internal links and SEO metadata.
- Add related articles and recommended reading sections.

### New Content Optimization

Every page should include:

- Optimized page title
- Engaging introduction
- Comprehensive content
- Benefits
- Features
- Step-by-step process
- Use cases
- Industry insights
- Best practices
- Common mistakes
- Frequently Asked Questions
- Key Takeaways
- Strong Call-to-Action
- Related Services
- Related Resources

### Internal Linking Strategy

Implement a comprehensive internal linking structure across the entire website.

Every page should:

- Link to relevant service pages.
- Link to related blog posts.
- Link to location pages where relevant.
- Link to industry pages.
- Link to case studies.
- Link to portfolio items.
- Link to the Contact page.
- Link to the About page where appropriate.
- Link to supporting resources.

Each page should include **10–20 relevant internal links** where appropriate, using natural anchor text.

Avoid orphan pages.

Build strong topical clusters and content silos.

### FAQ Requirements

Every important page must include a dedicated FAQ section.

Requirements:

- Minimum **10–20 unique FAQs** per page.
- FAQs must be relevant to the page topic.
- Questions should be based on real user search intent.
- Answers should be detailed, clear, and optimized for featured snippets and AI-generated search results.
- Implement valid FAQ Schema (JSON-LD).
- Avoid duplicate FAQs across different pages.

- Other AI-powered search engines

Implement:

### Content Structure

Every page should follow a logical structure using:

- H1 (one per page)
- H2 sections
- H3 subsections
- Bullet points
- Numbered lists
- Comparison tables
- Process diagrams (where appropriate)
- Statistics and facts
- Visual content recommendations
- Quote blocks
- Key Takeaways
- FAQ Section
- Strong CTA

### Blog Content

Every blog post should:

- Contain at least **2,000 words** (or more where justified by search intent).
- Be optimized for one primary keyword and related semantic keywords.
- Include internal links to service pages and related blogs.
- Include external links to authoritative sources where appropriate.
- Include FAQs and FAQ Schema.
- Include images with optimized ALT text.
- Include a conclusion and CTA.

### Content Quality Standards

Content should be:

- Human-first
- Easy to read
- Conversational
- Professional
- Actionable
- Factually accurate
- Well researched
- Free from grammatical errors
- Optimized for search intent
- Optimized for conversions
- Optimized for AI search

### Final Content Goal

The entire website should become a comprehensive topical authority in Digital Marketing, SEO, AI Search Optimization (AEO, LLMO, GEO), PPC, Google Business Profile, Social Media Marketing, Website Design & Development, Web Applications, Mobile Apps, SaaS Development, and MVP Development.

Every page, blog, service, and resource should be interconnected through a strong internal linking strategy, supported by comprehensive, high-quality content and unique FAQs, ensuring maximum visibility in Google Search, Google AI Overviews, featured snippets, People Also Ask, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot.
